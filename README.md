## Academic honesty

This repo contains **no problem set solutions**. CS50's [academic honesty policy](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/honesty/) asks students not to make their solutions available to others, so problem sets are submitted through CS50's own tools only. Reflections describe what I learned, not how to solve the problems. My final project will be shared here once it's done.

## About the notes

Lecture notes are the lesson material from my study sessions with Claude, kept as a quick reference. Reflections and experiments are my own work.
